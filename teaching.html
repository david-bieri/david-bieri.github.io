Teaching

Courses at the intersection of public finance, monetary theory and the history of economic thought, taught with open materials and a preference for making students argue with the data.

Current courses

Virginia Tech, School of Public and International Affairs

REAL / UAP 2004 — Principles of Real Estate

Markets, finance, valuation and the macroeconomy of property, with live dashboards on energy, sustainability and the cost of building. Open materials at real-estate-macro.

Undergraduate · spring

UAP 5174 — Planning Theory and History

From the German location theorists and the New Deal regionalists to the credibility revolution: how ideas about place became policy, and what survived the trip across the Atlantic.

Graduate · spring

Quantitative Methods for Urban and Regional Research

Causal inference and spatial econometrics for first-year graduate students, bilingual R and Python. The course is the seed of The Credibility of Place and of the interactive models at circular-flows.org.

Graduate

Advising

Doctoral and master's supervision on supreme audit institutions and sovereignty, urban climate-risk planning, and the political economy of money and finance.
