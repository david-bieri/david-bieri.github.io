Biography

David Bieri works at the intersection of regional economics, public finance and economic geography. His research asks how monetary and regulatory systems leave durable marks on places: how the Basel capital rules redrew the geography of financial regulation, how financial lock-in transformed the American South twice, why housing finance reform keeps stalling in a temporal trap, and how reserve-currency dynamics, correspondent banking and financial sanctions give the international monetary system its structural persistence.

A second strand of his work is the history of economic ideas, with a particular focus on German-speaking economists of the interwar period. He is the editor of August Lösch: Briefe / Letters, 1927–1945 (Metropolis Verlag, forthcoming), a 1,076-page critical edition of the correspondence of the founder of modern spatial economics, the product of more than a decade of archival work in Germany, Switzerland and the United States. His 2026–27 sabbatical project, From the New South to the New Europe, traces how German business-cycle research and location theory circulated into American regionalism and New Deal reform, and were re-exported to postwar Europe.

Before entering the academy he held senior positions at the Bank for International Settlements in Basel, the central bankers' bank, and worked in investment banking in London. That experience shapes his public commentary: he is a frequent source for national and regional press on tariffs and trade, the Federal Reserve, inflation, housing and energy shocks, and has been quoted by The Wall Street Journal, The Washington Post, TIME, Newsweek, CNN, CBS News, the Associated Press and Marketplace, among others.

He teaches principles of real estate, planning theory and history, and quantitative methods for urban and regional research, and is writing a graduate methods textbook, The Credibility of Place, that brings the credibility revolution in econometrics to spatial analysis. He directs the Political Space Economy Lab, whose digital home is circular-flows.org, and maintains august-loesch.org, an open archive for Lösch scholarship.

Short bio for programmes and press

David Bieri is Associate Professor of Urban Affairs in the School of Public and International Affairs at Virginia Tech, where he works on regional economics, monetary economics and the history of economic thought. A former senior official at the Bank for International Settlements, he is the editor of the collected letters of August Lösch (Metropolis, forthcoming) and a regular commentator on tariffs, the Fed and the economy for outlets including Marketplace, CBS News, TIME and Newsweek.
