David Bieri

Urban and real estate economist working where money, credit and property markets meet: a former central banker’s view of land, housing and regional development.

Associate Professor of Urban Affairs, School of Public and International Affairs, Virginia Tech · Associate Professor of Economics, by courtesy · faculty affiliate, Blackwood Department of Real Estate

    In brief

    Swiss-born, trained in Durham and at Virginia Tech, seven years in central banking at the BIS, and a decade and a half in the American academy. Research on how monetary and regulatory systems leave durable marks on property markets and regions — from housing finance and Basel capital rules to the financial history of the U.S. South — and on the German economists who first asked those questions.

    August Lösch and German spatial economics

    A decade in the archives: the complete letters of August Lösch (Metropolis, forthcoming), and the transatlantic transmission of German Konjunkturforschung into American regionalism — the sabbatical book, 2026–27.

    The institutional architecture of international money

    Reserve currency dynamics, correspondent banking, central bank independence and financial sanctions — the questions that began at the BIS and now shape public commentary on the Fed and the dollar.
